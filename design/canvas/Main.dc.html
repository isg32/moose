MOOSE — SCREENS v1
A quiet, private chat for two. Same language as Veronica, Idris and Cariño: only #000 and #FFF for the interface, Doto for titles and numbers, Inter for everything else. The couple's shared wallpaper is the one place colour comes in, and it decides the ink: dark wallpapers get white ink, light ones black.
RULES
1 · Two inks, no greys. Hierarchy comes from size, weight, outline vs fill, dashed vs solid, and inversion.
2 · Translucency only on the glass surfaces below. Disabled actions are the whole element at 0.35.
3 · Every screen sits on the shared wallpaper. Full pages (settings, sheets) lay a veil and a 30 px blur over it.
4 · Cariño's progressive blur at the top (under the status bar) and bottom (behind the composer and pill).
5 · Gutter 16 for cards, 20 for titles. Radii: card 20, bubble 20 (tail corner 6), sheet 28, pill 999.
6 · Icons are a 1.5 px round-cap line set on a 24 px grid. No emoji in the chrome; emoji belong to messages.
7 · Every screen board draws a dark-ink row and a light-ink row. Its tweaks pick the dark and light wallpaper.
8 · The one exception: moment colours. Hugs, pokes, kisses and miss-yous get a warm colour, drifting hearts and their emoji floating up. Only in moments, the poke picker and poke notifications.
